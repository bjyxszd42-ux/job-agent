Work authorization

Almost every US application asks these two questions separately. They are not the same question, and getting them wrong is one of the most common reasons applications get auto-rejected.

The two-question trap. "Are you legally authorized to work in the US?" and "Will you now or in the future require sponsorship?" are independent. An F-1 student on OPT is authorized to work right now (Yes) but will need sponsorship later (Yes). Answering "No" to the second question because you can work today is a misrepresentation and a common self-inflicted rejection.

Experience

Write more bullets than fit on a résumé — five to eight per role. This is a library, not the résumé itself. When you target a specific job, the right subset gets selected and reordered. That's why one honest record can produce a data-analytics résumé and a product résumé without rewriting anything.

Write bullets with the XYZ formula. "Accomplished [X] as measured by [Y] by doing [Z]." "Rebuilt the weekly reporting pipeline in dbt, cutting refresh time from 6 hours to 20 minutes for a team of 40" beats "Responsible for reporting" every time. Quantify whatever you honestly can.

Projects

Course projects, side projects, anything you built. For students these often carry more weight than a short internship, because they show what you choose to do when nobody assigns it.

Context / Challenge / Outcome reads well here. What was the problem worth solving, what constraint made it hard, and what did you actually ship or decide. The constraint is the part most people leave out, and it's the part that shows judgment.

Leadership & activities

Teaching assistant, club officer, volunteering. Same shape as a role — it gets its own section on the résumé.

Skills

Comma-separated. Used for keyword matching against job descriptions — only list what you'd be comfortable being asked about.

Voluntary self-identification (EEO)

US employers with federal contracts are required to invite this information, and equally required to let you decline. It is kept separate from hiring decisions. Every field below defaults to "decline" — nothing is pre-answered for you.

Never enter an SSN, driver's licence, or bank details here — or in any application. Legitimate employers only need those after an offer, through their HR system. A posting that asks for them at the application stage is almost certainly a scam.
